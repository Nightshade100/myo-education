Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

MYO Education doesn't collect, store or share any personal information through this website.

Your answers stay with you

Your answers to the symptom checker stay in your browser while the page is open. They're never sent to us or anyone else, and they're cleared when you close or refresh the page.

Your PDF summary

If you download a PDF, it's created on your own device. Anything you add to it, like your child's name, stays in that file.

No cookies or tracking

We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. As with any website, the services that host the site and its fonts (GitHub Pages and Google Fonts) receive basic technical details, such as your IP address, so they can show you the page.

Questions

If you have any questions about privacy, email us at myoscreening@gmail.com.
