Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

MYO Education is run by Inspire Doctors. This policy explains the only personal information we collect through this website, and what we do with it.

Your name and email

Before you see your symptom checker results, we ask for a name and an email address. When you submit them, they're saved in a private Google Sheet that only our team can access, along with the date and time and the fact that you agreed to this policy.

We use them so Inspire Doctors can contact you about the symptom checker and our services. We don't sell or share them with anyone else. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time using the email address below.

Your answers stay with you

Your answers to the symptom checker are never sent to us or anyone else, and they aren't stored anywhere. They stay in your browser while the page is open and are cleared when you close or refresh the page.

Your PDF summary

If you download a PDF, it's created on your own device. Anything you add to it, like your child's name, stays in that file.

No cookies or tracking

We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. As with any website, the services that host the site and its fonts (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare and Google Fonts) receive basic technical details, such as your IP address, so they can show you the page. Google also processes the name and email you submit, because it hosts the Google Sheet they're saved in.

Questions

If you have any questions about privacy, email us at hello@inspiredoctors.com.au.
