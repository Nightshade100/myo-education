Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

The short version

  • You can use the whole symptom checker without giving us any personal details. If you choose Skip for now, nothing is sent anywhere.
  • If you ask us to email your summary, we send it to you and keep a copy so our team can answer your questions.
  • We don't sell your information, use it for advertising or add you to a mailing list.
  • You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Who we are

This website is run by TO CONFIRM: legal or business name, and ABN ("MYO Education", "we", "us"). You can contact us about privacy at myoscreening@gmail.com or TO CONFIRM: postal address or phone number.

What we collect

If you only browse or skip the email step: your answers stay in your browser tab. They're kept there (in the browser's temporary "session storage") so a refresh doesn't lose your progress, and they're deleted when you close the tab or press Start over. We never receive them.

If you ask us to email your summary, we collect:

  • your name and email address
  • your child's first name and age
  • the signs your answers point to, and the "main concern" you type (if any)
  • the date and time you sent it.

Information about a child's health is sensitive, so we only collect it with your agreement, which you give by ticking the box on the form.

We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Like any website, the services that deliver our pages (GitHub Pages for the site, Google Fonts for the typefaces, and jsDelivr for the email library) receive basic technical information such as your IP address so they can send the files to your browser.

Why we collect it

  • To email you the summary you asked for, so you can take it to your GP or health practitioner.
  • To let our team answer questions if you get in touch about your child's results.

We don't use it for anything else unless you ask us to, or the law requires it.

Who receives it

  • You, at the email address you enter.
  • The MYO Education team, who receive a copy of the same email.
  • EmailJS, the service that sends the email from our website, and Google, which provides our email account. Both may store information on servers outside Australia.
  • Google reCAPTCHA, if the "I'm not a robot" check is shown, which helps stop automated misuse of the form.

How long we keep it

We keep emailed summaries for TO CONFIRM: for example 12 months and then delete them, unless your child becomes a patient, in which case it may be kept as part of their health record.

Your choices

  • Access or correction: ask us for a copy of what we hold, or to correct it.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your summary and details. Reply to your summary email or write to the address above.
  • Complaints: contact us first and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
