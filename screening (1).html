Orofacial Screener — O.A.S

Red Flags for Paediatric Sleep Disordered Breathing

A weighted, evidence-informed screening tool to identify risk indicators for paediatric sleep disordered breathing (pSDB). This tool is a clinical screener — not a diagnosis.

⚠ Important: This is a screening tool only. It does not diagnose any condition. A full diagnosis can only be made after a clinical examination. Designed for children aged 4–17.

How this screener works

  1. 1.Answer Yes or No to each question across 6 sections.
  2. 2.Each "Yes" answer adds a weighted score indicating risk for paediatric sleep disordered breathing.
  3. 3.Your total weighted score determines the risk level (0–14 low, 15–29 mild, 30–49 moderate, 50+ high).
  4. 4.Your personalised report will be sent to our team, who will contact you with guidance.

Risk Grading Scale

Based on a weighted, evidence-informed paediatric sleep-disordered breathing scoring system

Certain combinations of high-risk indicators (e.g. witnessed apnoea + snoring + mouth breathing) will automatically elevate the risk level regardless of the total score.
