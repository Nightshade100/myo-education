Orofacial symptom checker — O.A.S

Red flags for paediatric sleep disordered breathing

A simple checklist to help you notice common signs of paediatric sleep disordered breathing (pSDB). This symptom checker is not a diagnosis.

Important: This is a symptom checker only. It does not diagnose any condition. A full diagnosis can only be made after a clinical examination. Designed for children aged 4–17.

How this symptom checker works

  1. Answer Yes or No to each question across 6 sections. Tap the ⓘ beside a question if you're not sure what to look for.
  2. At the end you'll see a list of any signs your answers point to. If there are any, we recommend your child is seen by a GP or qualified health practitioner.
  3. If none of your answers point to a sign, no further action is needed.
  4. You can download your results as a PDF to take to your appointment. Your answers aren't sent anywhere or saved — see our privacy policy.
